How It Works

Three easy steps from sandy shells to a shiny keepsake.

🐚
Step 1

Collect your shells

Walk the beach and pick up your favorite shells and clam shells.

📦
Step 2

Send them to us

Pop them in an envelope with a note about your beach day and mail them in.

🏺
Step 3

Get your glass keepsake

We arrange them in sand inside glass and send your one-of-a-kind piece home.
